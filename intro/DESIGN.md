# We Better Intro Design

## Style Prompt

Clean, modern, and warm. Designed to instill a sense of calm, growth, and forward momentum. Uses soft palettes and elegant typography to elevate the self-improvement message.

## Colors

- `--bg-primary`: `#FCFAFC` (Soft warm white)
- `--bg-secondary`: `#F3F0F4` (Muted violet-gray)
- `--accent-primary`: `#6B4C8A` (Deep calming purple)
- `--accent-secondary`: `#E8A598` (Soft energetic coral)
- `--text-primary`: `#1A1620` (Off-black)
- `--text-muted`: `#746E7B` (Soft gray)

## Typography

- Primary: `"Inter", sans-serif` (Clean, modern, legible)
- Display: `"Outfit", sans-serif` (Friendly, geometric)

## What NOT to Do

- Do NOT use harsh pure black (`#000000`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for large areas.
- Do NOT use aggressive, fast, or chaotic animations. Everything must feel deliberate and calm.
- Do NOT clutter the frame. Leave ample white space.
